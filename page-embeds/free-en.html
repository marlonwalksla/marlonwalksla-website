Explore with Ernesto

Ernesto, our piñata donkey, hides along the route as stickers. Spot him, snap a photo, and keep count. Whoever finds the most Ernestos wins a signed Polaroid. Every guest takes home a free Ernesto sticker.

Questions

Is the tour really free?

Yes. Booking costs nothing. At the end, you tip what you feel the tour was worth.

How long is it, and how far do we walk?

2 to 2.5 hours and 1.8 miles (2.9 km), with stops along the way.

Where does it end?

At Placita Olvera and El Pueblo de Los Ángeles, a short walk from Union Station and the Metro.

Is it good for kids?

Yes. Families tell us the Ernesto sticker hunt keeps kids busy the whole way.

What should I bring?

Comfortable shoes and water. On sunny days, sunscreen, a hat or an umbrella for shade.

What if not enough people book?

The tour runs with 3 or more guests. If 2 or fewer book, Marlon may cancel and will email you at least 24 hours before.

Is the route accessible?

There are stairs at Angels Flight. Coming with a wheelchair or stroller? Message Marlon before the tour.

Can I bring a big group?

For more than 10 people, or if you want a private experience, book a team tour or a private tour.
