Why I guide

As a son of LA, I want to share my love for my city. People think of LA as freeways and movie stars, but Downtown is where the city began, and most visitors (and plenty of locals) have never really explored it.

The best part of my job is meeting people from all over the world and showing them the best of my city. Guests have joined me from Spain, Argentina, Mexico, Chile, Peru, Germany, Italy, Portugal, Singapore, Canada and all over the US.

Meet Ernesto

Ernesto is a piñata donkey and the heart of every tour. He hides along the route as stickers, and guests compete to find the most. Add Ernesto's story: where the idea came from and why a piñata

It makes me smile seeing how much grown adults enjoy competing to find Ernesto, because we're all kids on the inside.

The Polaroid tradition

In November 2025 I took my first Polaroid of a guest. It meant so much that I decided every tour should feel fun and special, so now the winner of the Ernesto hunt takes home a signed Polaroid, and team and private groups get one too. Lately guests write on them and add Ernesto stickers.
