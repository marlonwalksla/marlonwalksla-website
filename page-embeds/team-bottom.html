Questions from planners

How far do we walk?

About 1.8 miles (2.9 km) over about 6 hours, with breaks and a long lunch. The pace is easy and the route can be shortened.

Can you start at our office?

Yes. Marlon meets your group at your office or hotel in Downtown LA.

Can the tour be in Spanish?

Yes. Marlon guides in full English or full Spanish.

Is lunch included?

No. We stop for lunch at Grand Central Market, where everyone picks from 30+ food stalls.

Do you invoice companies?

Yes. You'll get an invoice, itemized receipts and a W-9.

What if it rains or is very hot?

If extreme heat or rain is on the way, Marlon messages your group before the tour and you decide together.

How far ahead should we book?

At least 1 week ahead.

What's the largest group you take?

Up to 30 guests. Bigger group? Message Marlon.

Give your team a day out in LA

Fresh air, real conversation and a team Polaroid to take back to the office.
