Questions from planners

How far do we walk?

About 1.6 miles (2.6 km) in a loop over about 5 hours, with breaks and lunch at the end. We ride Angels Flight up Bunker Hill, so the rest is mostly downhill or flat.

Where do we meet?

At Grand Central Market (317 S Broadway). We start and finish there, so lunch is the last stop.

Do we get photos?

Yes. Marlon brings an instant camera that the group passes around, with enough film for everyone to leave with a printed photo.

Can the tour be in Spanish?

Yes. Marlon guides in full English or full Spanish.

Is lunch included?

Lunch is not in the tour price. We stop at Grand Central Market, where everyone picks their own food from 30+ stalls. Prefer catering? Marlon can arrange it for your group and confirms the lunch price with you.

Do you invoice companies?

Yes. You'll get an invoice, itemized receipts and a W-9.

What if it rains or is very hot?

If extreme heat or rain is on the way, Marlon messages your group before the tour and you decide together.

How far ahead should we book?

At least 1 week ahead.

What's the largest group you take?

Up to 35 guests. The price calculator above goes to 20; for 21 to 35, Marlon works out a better price per person with you: message Marlon or email marlonwalksla@gmail.com.

Give your team a day out in LA

Fresh air, real conversation and photos to take back to the office.
