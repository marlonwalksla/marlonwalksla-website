Privacy Policy

Effective date: add the date this goes live

MarlonWalksLA ("we", "us") runs walking tours in Los Angeles and this website, marlonwalksla.com. This page explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions? Email marlonwalksla@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • When you book a free tour: your name, email and the details you enter in the booking calendar (run by Cal.com).
  • When you send a team or private tour inquiry: the details you type in the form, such as your name, email, company, group size, preferred date, language and message.
  • When you message us on WhatsApp: your phone number and the messages you send.
  • When you browse the site: basic usage data such as pages visited, device and approximate location, collected by Google Analytics using cookies.
  • The trip planner on our map: spots you save, places you mark as visited and any profile details you enter are stored only in your own browser. They are not sent to us.

How we use it

  • To confirm, remind you about and run your tour
  • To reply to your questions and send quotes for team and private tours
  • To tell you if a tour is changed or cancelled
  • To understand how people use the site so we can improve it

We do not sell your information. Do you send newsletters or promotions? If yes, say so here and how people unsubscribe.

Services we use

These companies handle some of your information on our behalf, under their own privacy policies: Webflow (website and forms), Cal.com (bookings), Google Analytics (site statistics), Google Maps and Mapbox (maps), and WhatsApp (messages). If you book through GuruWalk, Civitatis, FreeTour.com or Viator, that platform's privacy policy applies to your booking. Confirm this list is complete (email tool? payment tool for private tours?)

Photos on tour

We take photos and Polaroids during tours. We only share photos of guests on our website or social media with their permission.

Cookies

Google Analytics uses cookies to count visits. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and the site will still work.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we have about you by emailing marlonwalksla@gmail.com. We keep booking and inquiry details only as long as we need them to run tours and keep basic records. How long do you keep them (for example, 2 years)?

Children

Families are welcome on our tours, but this website is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information online.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
