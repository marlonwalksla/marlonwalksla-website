Questions

How does pricing work?

Per group, up to 4 guests. The more people you bring and the longer the tour, the less each person pays per hour. Hotel pickup, drop-off and all the driving are included.

How many people can come?

Up to 4 guests in the car. Bigger group? See our team tours.

Where can you pick us up?

Marlon picks you up at your hotel and drops you off at your hotel.

Can we change the stops?

Yes. The tours above are starting points. Tell Marlon what matters to you and he'll plan around it.

Is it good for kids and grandparents?

Yes. The pace is set by your group, and the Ernesto photo hunt keeps kids busy.

Do you have car seats?

No, we don't provide car seats.

How do we pay?

Cash or digital payments: credit card, Venmo, PayPal or Revolut.

What's your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 48 hours before your tour. Cancel later than that and 50% of the price is charged.
