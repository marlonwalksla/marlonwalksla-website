Good to know

What is included?

Your guide and instant photos. Private tours also include hotel pickup, drop-off and all the driving. Parking, tickets and food at stops are not included.

How many people can come?

Private tours: up to 4 guests in the car. Group walks: 5 to 35 guests.

Can we change the stops?

Yes. Every tour is a starting point. Tell Marlon what matters to you and he plans around it.

Is it in Spanish?

Yes. Every tour runs fully in English or fully in Spanish.

Is it good for kids and grandparents?

Yes. Your group sets the pace, and the Ernesto photo hunt keeps kids busy. We don't provide car seats.

How do we pay, and can we cancel?

Cash, card, Venmo, PayPal or Revolut. Free cancellation up to 48 hours before. Later than that, 50% is charged.
