Free Walking Tour of Downtown LA

Come walk LA's historic downtown. Start at Bunker Hill, home of LA's arts buildings, then walk backwards in time to Placita Olvera, where the Spanish and Mexican pueblo began.

English tour at 12:30 PM and Spanish tour at 9:30 AM. Meet at Walt Disney Concert Hall, 600 W 1st St. Free to book, tip what feels right at the end. The walk takes 2 to 2.5 hours and covers about 1.8 miles (2.9 km) on foot. Everyone gets a free Ernesto sticker. Food, drinks and parking are not included.

Team Building Walking Tours

Get out of the office and explore Downtown LA together with your team on a loop that starts and ends at Grand Central Market. Learn some local history, breathe in some fresh air, and break bread together.

Pricing for 5 to 20 guests: for example about $790 for 15 people (about $53 per person). The price per person goes down as the group grows. Lunch is either bought by each guest at Grand Central Market, or Marlon arranges catering for your group.

Good to Know

Where do we meet?

Free tour: Walt Disney Concert Hall, 600 W 1st St. Look for Marlon with a tablet. Team tour: Grand Central Market, 317 S Broadway. Private tour: Marlon picks you up at your hotel.

What about parking?

The free tour is on foot, so you only park once. Private tours include hotel pickup, so you do not need to park at all. Want garage tips? Message Marlon.

Is the route accessible?

There are stairs at Angels Flight. Coming with a wheelchair or stroller? Message Marlon before the tour and we will plan it.

How long is the free tour?

About 2 hours, in English or Spanish. It is free to book. Tip what feels right at the end.

What if I need to cancel?

Free tours run with 3 or more guests. If you cannot make it, email at least 24 hours before. Private tours cancel free up to 48 hours before.

What if it is hot or rainy?

For team tours, Marlon messages the group and you decide together.
