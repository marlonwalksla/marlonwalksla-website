Pick your date, see the route

Tour dates change each month. Booking is free and takes under a minute. The walk goes from modern Bunker Hill down to the 1781 birthplace of the city: 2 to 2.5 hours and 1.8 miles (2.9 km). Follow Ernesto from stop to stop, or tap a stop to jump there.

Tour stops

  • Walt Disney Concert Hall: Where we meet. Frank Gehry's steel landmark and the story of how it got built.
  • The Broad: Next door, a free contemporary art museum that opened in 2015.
  • Angels Flight: Built in 1901, "the world's shortest railway" has carried over 100 million passengers.
  • Grand Central Market: Open since 1917. Today it's 30+ food stalls from all over the world.
  • Bradbury Building: The 1893 interior you've seen in movies.
  • The Evolution of Los Angeles: The story of LA on one mural.
  • City Hall: The 1928 tower at the heart of LA's government.
  • The courthouses: LA's courthouses and the stories behind them.
  • Placita Olvera and El Pueblo de Los Ángeles: Where Los Angeles began in 1781. The tour ends here, a short walk from Union Station.

Collect stamps as you walk

Open your free Downtown passport on your phone. Tap a stamp at each stop, then send a postcard home. No account, no app. Your stamps also show on the passport map.

Explore with Ernesto

Ernesto, our piñata donkey, hides along the route as stickers. Spot him, snap a photo, and keep count. Whoever finds the most Ernestos wins a signed Polaroid. Every guest takes home a free Ernesto sticker.

Kids love it. So do grown-ups: one guest found 67 Ernestos in a single walk.

Questions

Is the tour really free?

Yes. Booking costs nothing. At the end, you tip what you feel the tour was worth.

How long is it, and how far do we walk?

2 to 2.5 hours and 1.8 miles (2.9 km), with stops along the way.

Where does it end?

At Placita Olvera and El Pueblo de Los Ángeles, a short walk from Union Station and the Metro.

Is it good for kids?

Yes. Families tell us the Ernesto sticker hunt keeps kids busy the whole way.

What should I bring?

Comfortable shoes and water. On sunny days, sunscreen, a hat or an umbrella for shade.

What if not enough people book?

The tour runs with 3 or more guests. If 2 or fewer book, Marlon may cancel and will email you at least 24 hours before.

Is the route accessible?

There are stairs at Angels Flight. Coming with a wheelchair or stroller? Message Marlon before the tour.

Can I bring a big group?

For more than 10 people, or if you want a private experience, book a team tour or a private tour.
