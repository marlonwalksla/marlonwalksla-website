1. Griffith Observatory. The best view of LA, with the Hollywood Sign on one side and the city on the other. Free to get in. Come near sunset for the city lights.
  2. Hollywood Walk of Fame. More than 2,700 stars line Hollywood Boulevard. Look down. Find a star for someone you love.
  3. Hollywood Sign view. See the sign up close from the quiet park below it. The park at Lake Hollywood has a clear view and a dog park.
  4. TCL Chinese Theatre. The 1927 movie palace with the stars' handprints out front. Put your hands in the famous cement prints in the front yard.
  5. Santa Monica Pier. Ferris wheel, ocean air and the end of Route 66. Walk to the end of the pier and look back at the coast.
  6. Venice Beach Boardwalk. LA's most colorful boardwalk: skaters, artists and Muscle Beach. Skaters, muscle beach and street art. Explore it slowly.
  7. The Getty Center. Art, gardens and big views from a hilltop, free to get in. Free to get in. Parking costs money, so take the tram up the hill.
  8. Rodeo Drive. Three famous blocks of Beverly Hills shops and photo spots. Window shopping is free. Walk it and enjoy the people watching.
  9. Walt Disney Concert Hall. Frank Gehry's steel waves, with a garden hidden up the stairs. The steel curves look different from every side. Walk all the way around.
  10. The Broad. Modern art next door to Disney Hall, free with a timed ticket. Free to get in, but book a time online. The line outside moves fast.
  11. Grand Central Market. LA's food hall since 1917. Come hungry. Share a few things from different stalls. Explore before you buy.
  12. Olvera Street. Where Los Angeles began in 1781, with music, papel picado and taquitos. This is where LA started. Try the taquitos and look for the papel picado.
